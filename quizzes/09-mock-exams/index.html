Mock exams

Six full-length, timed 60-question practice exams — 360 questions total, shaped exactly like the real CCA-F: same five domains, same weighting, same 2-hour pausable clock, score withheld until you submit.

Different from the section quizzes above. Those drill one topic at a time with instant feedback. These simulate exam day: no feedback until the end, a question palette, flag-for-review, and a per-domain score breakdown afterward.

Completion is tracked automatically — each card below shows your attempts and best score, pulled from this browser. Connect gist sync (below) once per device to carry that across desktop and mobile.
