# 01 · AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Quiz

### Q1. Name the three modes of engaging AI covered in "Why do we need AI Fluency?"
---
**A:** Automation (fully delegate, little human involvement in execution), Augmentation (AI assists, human stays in the loop and retains control), Agency (AI acts with independent initiative toward a goal).
*Memory hook:* Picture a three-lane road: **autopilot**, **co-pilot**, and a self-driving taxi choosing its own route.

### Q2. Name the four competencies in the 4D Framework for AI Fluency, and who developed it.
---
**A:** Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence. Developed by Prof. Rick Dakan (Ringling College) and Prof. Joseph Feller (University College Cork), licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
*Memory hook:* Keep four **D-shaped drawers** in your mental desk: **Decide**, **Describe**, **Double-check**, and **Do-right**.

### Q3. Which 4D competency is about deciding what work should go to a human vs. to AI?
---
**A:** Delegation.
*Memory hook:* Delegation is the airport controller deciding which passenger belongs on the human flight and which can board the AI shuttle.

### Q4. Which 4D competency is about critically evaluating AI outputs for accuracy and quality before relying on them?
---
**A:** Discernment.
*Memory hook:* Discernment is an editor with a red pen circling the AI's confident claim and asking, “Show me the receipt.”

### Q5. Which 4D competency covers using AI responsibly, ethically, with transparency and accountability?
---
**A:** Diligence.
*Memory hook:* Diligence is the seatbelt and safety inspection you buckle before letting an AI-generated idea onto the road.

### Q6. List the six effective prompting techniques from this course.
---
**A:** Give context, show examples, specify constraints, break into steps, ask the AI to think first, define role/tone.
*Memory hook:* Pack a prompt toolbox with a **map**, **sample photo**, **guardrails**, **staircase**, **thinking pause**, and **costume badge**.

### Q7. What's the "secret weapon" technique beyond the six core ones?
---
**A:** Ask the AI to improve your own prompt before you use it — it's often better at refining prompt instructions than a first human draft.
*Memory hook:* Before ordering dinner, ask the chef to rewrite your messy order into a recipe the kitchen cannot misunderstand.

### Q8. An agentic coding tool like Claude Code, operating toward a goal with its own judgment about how to get there, best exemplifies which of the three AI engagement modes?
---
**A:** Agency.
*Memory hook:* Agency is giving a dog a destination and watching it choose the streets, detours, and sniff-stops to get there.

### Q9. Which 4D competency does "showing an example of the format you want" most directly draw on, and why?
---
**A:** Description — it's about communicating effectively with the AI and defining the desired output, of which examples are one technique.
*Memory hook:* If you want a sandwich made your way, a photo of the perfect sandwich often beats a paragraph about “roughly this much lettuce.”

### Q10. What three awareness checks support good Delegation?
---
**A:** Problem Awareness (understand the goal, audience, success criteria, and constraints), Platform Awareness (understand the AI system's capabilities and limits), and Task Delegation (deliberately divide the work between human and AI).
*Memory hook:* Before handing over a map, ask: **Where are we going? What vehicle do we have? Who drives which leg?**

### Q11. What are the three dimensions of a good Description?
---
**A:** Product Description defines the desired deliverable, Process Description defines the method or steps, and Performance Description defines how the AI should collaborate and respond to feedback.
*Memory hook:* Plan a trip with a **destination postcard** (product), a **route map** (process), and a **travel companion brief** (performance).

### Q12. How should Discernment evaluate an AI output across the same three dimensions?
---
**A:** Check the product for accuracy, relevance, completeness, and audience fit; inspect the process for logical steps, visible assumptions, and appropriate evidence; and assess performance for adherence to the requested terminology, tone, boundaries, and feedback.
*Memory hook:* Inspect the **dish**, the **kitchen**, and the **waiter**—is the food right, was it prepared sensibly, and did the service follow your instructions?

### Q13. What is the Description–Discernment loop?
---
**A:** Define the desired product, process, and collaboration behavior, evaluate the result across those same dimensions, then refine the request and repeat until the output is fit for purpose.
*Memory hook:* Think of a potter's wheel: shape the clay, inspect the bowl, press the rough spot, and spin it again.

### Q14. Name the three forms of Diligence and the responsibility each emphasizes.
---
**A:** Creation Diligence concerns choosing systems, tools, data, and workflows responsibly; Transparency Diligence concerns disclosing AI's role when appropriate; and Deployment Diligence concerns verifying the result and personally owning what is shared or used.
*Memory hook:* Picture three locks on a launch gate: **choose a safe rocket**, **label the AI passenger**, and **inspect the payload before liftoff**.
