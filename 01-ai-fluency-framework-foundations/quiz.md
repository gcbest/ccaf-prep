# 01 · AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Quiz

### Q1. Name the three modes of engaging AI covered in "Why do we need AI Fluency?"
---
**A:** Automation (fully delegate, little human involvement in execution), Augmentation (AI assists, human stays in the loop and retains control), Agency (AI acts with independent initiative toward a goal).

### Q2. Name the four competencies in the 4D Framework for AI Fluency, and who developed it.
---
**A:** Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence. Developed by Prof. Rick Dakan (Ringling College) and Prof. Joseph Feller (University College Cork), licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

### Q3. Which 4D competency is about deciding what work should go to a human vs. to AI?
---
**A:** Delegation.

### Q4. Which 4D competency is about critically evaluating AI outputs for accuracy and quality before relying on them?
---
**A:** Discernment.

### Q5. Which 4D competency covers using AI responsibly, ethically, with transparency and accountability?
---
**A:** Diligence.

### Q6. List the six effective prompting techniques from this course.
---
**A:** Give context, show examples, specify constraints, break into steps, ask the AI to think first, define role/tone.

### Q7. What's the "secret weapon" technique beyond the six core ones?
---
**A:** Ask the AI to improve your own prompt before you use it — it's often better at refining prompt instructions than a first human draft.

### Q8. An agentic coding tool like Claude Code, operating toward a goal with its own judgment about how to get there, best exemplifies which of the three AI engagement modes?
---
**A:** Agency.

### Q9. Which 4D competency does "showing an example of the format you want" most directly draw on, and why?
---
**A:** Description — it's about communicating effectively with the AI and defining the desired output, of which examples are one technique.
