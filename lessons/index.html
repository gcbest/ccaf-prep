The Northstar dispatch

Nine lessons, one company, one continuous story. You are the architect at Northstar, and each lesson is a day where a decision has to be made before the work can continue.

Every lesson teaches one official CCA-F prep course through scenes rather than bullet lists. Wrong answers in the interactive scenes are written out too — the point is to understand why a plausible option is the wrong one, because that is the shape of the exam.

Suggested order: read them in file order. The story runs chronologically — lesson 2 happens before lesson 1 in Northstar time, then everything moves forward. If you are short on time, weight your review by the exam percentages shown on each card.

The lessons

Section 1 · AI Fluency The week Northstar decided how to work Before anyone picks a tool, the company has to answer a harder question: what work belongs to a person, what belongs to an AI, and how will anyone know the result is good? The 4D framework, and the three modes of engaging AI. Prompt Engineering & Structured Output · 20% Section 2 · Claude 101 The Northstar launch One busy day, a growing team, and a series of decisions that reveal what Claude is — and how to work with it well. Chat vs. Cowork vs. Code, Projects, Skills, Artifacts, connectors, and the lightweight eval loop. Prompt Engineering & Structured Output · 20% Section 3 · Part 1 of 3 The first integration Northstar leaves the Claude apps behind and builds a product on the API. One request path, and every parameter that decides whether the thing works: models, messages, system prompts, temperature, streaming, structured output, prompt engineering, and evals. Prompt Engineering & Structured Output · 20% Section 3 · Part 2 of 3 The day Northstar grew hands The product can already write beautifully and knows nothing recent. Today it learns to do things and to look things up: tool use and the agent loop, tool schema design, computer use, RAG and hybrid retrieval, context management, and prompt caching. Tool Design & MCP 18% · Context & Reliability 15% Section 3 · Part 3 of 3 The shape of the system One Claude call is no longer enough, and the question stops being “what do I send?” and becomes “what kind of system am I building?” Workflows vs. agents, the four workflow patterns, environment inspection, and managed agents. Agentic Architecture & Orchestration · 27% — the heaviest domain Section 4 · Amazon Bedrock The Cascade Mutual migration The biggest customer runs entirely on AWS and will not accept a second vendor path. The product has to move to Bedrock this quarter — without rewriting the product. What changes at the access layer, and what provably does not. Deployment options · scenario material Section 5 · Google Cloud The Meridian port The second big customer runs on Google Cloud. Same product, same prompts, same agent loop — and a completely different front door. Exactly which pieces move, and how Vertex differs from Bedrock on the same axes. Deployment options · scenario material Section 6 · MCP The Northstar adapter Northstar has been hand-building a connector for every service it touches. Today it stops, and builds one thing every surface can plug into. The three primitives — tools, resources, and prompts — and who controls each. Tool Design & MCP Integration · 18% Section 7 · Claude Code The keys to the repo Engineering is adopting Claude Code across a real payments codebase, and every hour someone asks for a little more autonomy than the last person. How much to grant, where the rules live, and what must never be automatic. Claude Code Configuration & Workflows · 20%
